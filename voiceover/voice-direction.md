# Voice Direction

CineForge projects use one signature voice for narration.

## Voice choice

- Engine/voice id: `avocado_v2:MAI_03` ("Smooth", gentle American male).
- Rationale: it stays listenable over 15 to 23 minutes of narration. Deeper "boomy" voices were tested and rejected because they tire the ear on long-form recap and story content.
- One project, one voice. Do not switch voices mid-series unless a character-dialogue pass demands it, and even then keep the narrator constant.

## Performance notation

Direction is baked into the script, not given to the voice actor (or the engine) separately. These marks control pacing:

### Ellipses (...) for suspense pauses

Write `...` where the narrator should hold a beat. Use before reveals, after shocking statements, and at scene turns.

Example:

> The cauldron was empty... or so he thought. Something pulsed beneath the ash.

Example:

> She turned the key, and the door swung open... to nothing. No, worse. To a staircase going down.

### Short hammer-blow sentences for impact

Break a beat into two or three staccato sentences when it should land hard.

Example:

> The flame died. The hall went dark. And then, from the far corner, a voice spoke.

Example:

> He had one choice. Fight. Or burn.

### Slow burn before reveals

Stack two or three setup lines of rising dread, then one plain line that names the thing. No shouting, no exclamation marks, just the quiet delivery.

Example:

> The wind stopped. The torches bent low, though no hand touched them. And the old man's eyes finally opened.

### Rising finish

End episodes and major acts on a climbing beat: shorter sentences, quickening rhythm, closing line that pulls toward the next episode.

Example:

> The gate is open. The beast is awake. And Xiao Yi... Xiao Yi is standing in its way.

## Spoken-form numbers

Write every number and time in spoken form. Never put digits in a script line, because the engine reads them inconsistently.

- "twenty-three minutes" not "23 minutes"
- "half past nine" not "9:30"
- "two thousand years" not "2000 years"
- "chapter forty-eight" not "chapter 48"

## Subscribe/like bumper rule

Every episode opens AND closes with a SHORT spoken narrator line prompting subscribe and like. A few seconds each. Spoken line only, no text overlay, no end card text. Keep it under ten words and do not let it touch the story content.

Examples:

- Opening: "If you want the next episode soon, hit subscribe."
- Closing: "Like the video, subscribe, and I will see you in the next one."
