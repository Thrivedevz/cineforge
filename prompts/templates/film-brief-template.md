# Film Brief Template

The project-level brief. Fill this once per film, before any scene brief is written. The style DNA block is pasted byte-identical into every scene brief's [Look] and [Output] so the whole film shares one finish.

---

## Filled Example (short)

**The Salt Road**

**Logline:** A retired courier crosses a drowned highway to deliver one last letter, while the tide takes the road behind her.

**Runtime target:** 6 minutes.

**Chapter / scene count:** 10 scenes, roughly 36 seconds each.

**Aspect ratio (locked):** 16:9.

**Visual medium (locked):** Photorealistic coastal drama.

## Style DNA

Overcast daylight with soft silver key, desaturated blue-grey palette, salt spray in the air in every exterior. Fine film grain. Slow dolly or locked tripod only.

## Character List

- **ILSA:** Retired courier, 60s, grey braid, oilskin coat. Lock: `characters/ilsa.md`.
- **BOY:** Ferry kid, 12, red cap. Lock: `characters/boy.md`.

## Location List

- **DROWNED HIGHWAY:** Dawn, flooded asphalt, abandoned cars, gulls.
- **FERRY DOCK:** Midday, wooden pilings, rope coils, grey water.

## Sound Direction

**Ambience bed:** Coastal wind, gull cries, water lapping. Continuous across all scenes.

**Music:** Solo cello, enters at scene 4, stays out of dialogue scenes.

**Dialogue:** Two voices: ILSA (low female, `studio:GRAVEL_01`), BOY (young male, `studio:BRIGHT_03`).

**Transitions:** Short dissolves, 12 frames. Background audio never drops between scenes.

## Delivery Spec

**Format:** MP4, H.264. **Resolution:** 1920x1080. **Bitrate cap:** 6 Mbps. **Audio:** Stereo, normalized. **File naming:** `the-salt-road-v1.mp4`, fresh filename per version.
