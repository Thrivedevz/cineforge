# Character Lock Template

Define once per character. Reference the lock by name in every scene brief instead of re-describing. If a detail is not in the lock, the generator will invent it differently every time.

---

## [CHARACTER NAME]

**Anchor name:** [SHORT HANDLE used in briefs, e.g. MARA]

**Art style:** [e.g. photorealistic, 2D anime clean linework. Must match the project's style DNA.]

**Face:** [age range, face shape, skin tone, distinguishing marks: scars, moles, tattoos. Be specific and physical.]

**Hair:** [color, length, style, how it moves. This is the highest-drift feature: lock it hard.]

**Build and posture:** [height impression, build, default posture, gait note.]

**Costume (immutable details):** [every garment, layer by layer. Name fabrics, colors, wear patterns, tears, fastenings. Mark which details must NEVER change with (LOCKED).]

**Signature prop or effect:** [weapon, tool, flame, glow: full design including color, shape, behavior. If it glows, the glow color is locked here.]

**Voice (if dialogue):** [voice id, pitch, pace, accent note.]

**What must never change:** [bullet list of the 3-6 details that define recognition: hair, costume pieces, marks, prop design, glow color.]

**Common drift to watch:** [the ways this character usually breaks: hair lengthening, costume colors shifting, age drifting, prop redesigning itself.]

## Filled Example (short)

**Anchor name:** MARA

**Art style:** Photorealistic dark fantasy.

**Face:** Mid 30s, square jaw, deep brown skin, thin scar through the left eyebrow.

**Hair:** Black, shaved on the sides, long braided top falling past the shoulders. The braid never shortens.

**Build and posture:** Tall, broad shoulders, stands with weight on one leg, walks with a slight limp favoring the left.

**Costume (immutable details):** Charcoal wool cloak with a burnt hem (LOCKED), leather cuirass with three brass buckles (LOCKED), grey linen shirt, dark trousers tucked into scuffed boots, iron ring on the right thumb.

**Signature prop or effect:** Hand-and-a-half sword, plain steel, leather-wrapped grip, no glow, small notch on the blade near the tip (LOCKED).

**Voice (if dialogue):** Low female, unhurried, faint northern accent.

**What must never change:** The side-shaved braided hair; the burnt cloak hem; the three brass buckles; the sword's blade notch; the scar through the left eyebrow.

**Common drift to watch:** Hair growing out the shaved sides; cloak turning black instead of charcoal; buckles changing count; the sword gaining ornamentation.
