## Filled Example (short)

[Output] 5 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic live-action look.

[Subject] JUNO as defined in the character lock ( courier, red jacket, messenger bag).

[World] 6:00 AM, empty subway car, fluorescent flicker. Steel poles foreground, Juno midground, dark tunnel windows behind. Cold recycled air.

[Action] Trigger: the train brakes hard. Continuous motion: Juno stumbles forward two steps, grabbing a pole, the messenger bag swinging off her shoulder. Visible result: she catches the bag one-handed before it hits the floor and exhales.

[Camera] Medium shot, eye level, slight handheld sway with the train's motion, ending frame: Juno upright, bag secured, pole in hand.

[Look] Flat fluorescent key, cool green-white grade, steel with worn texture. Handheld synced to the train's sway.

[Sound] Ambience: train rumble, brake squeal. Action sound: bag strap sliding, pole grab clang. Music: none. Dialogue: none.

[Constraints] Must remain: red jacket, messenger bag design, 16:9. Likely failure modes: Juno freezes mid-stumble; the bag passes through her arm; the train interior stretches. Reject any take where she stops moving before catching the bag.
