# Anime-Action Directing Briefs

Copy-ready scene briefs for anime-style action. Every brief follows the 8-block formula. All are model-agnostic.

## Effects Consistency Rules (apply to every brief below)

- Flames, energy, and weapons keep ONE locked look across every scene in a project. Define the flame design once (color core, edge behavior, smoke, spark pattern) in the character lock, then reference it. Never let the generator invent a new flame per scene.
- A weapon's shape, size, and ornamentation are immutable. If a blade glows, its glow color is locked. If a staff extends, its extended length is locked.
- Technique names stay identical in every brief. One name, one visual signature, no drift.
- Every scene must be real animation: continuous motion in every frame, no stills, no slow-zoom fallbacks over a frozen image, no "dramatic pause" that is actually a static frame. If the characters are not moving, the environment moves (dust, embers, cloth, hair) so the frame never dies.

## Brief 1: Duel, Bamboo Forest

[Output] 7 seconds, 16:9, 2D anime style, clean linework, dynamic shading.

[Subject] REN as defined in the character lock (long black ponytail, teal swordsman robe, twin short blades, locked design). KAZE as defined in the character lock (white topknot, crimson haori, single long katana, locked design).

[World] Dawn, bamboo forest, light fog between the stalks. Bamboo foreground framing the fighters, cut stalks midground, pale sun shafts slanting through. Fallen leaves drifting in the air currents.

[Action] Trigger: a single leaf touches the ground between them. Continuous motion: both draw in the same instant, Ren's twin blades sweeping low in crossing arcs while Kaze's katana cuts downward; bamboo stalks shear and topple in the blade paths, leaves bursting upward. Visible result: the blades lock at the center, sparks scattering, both fighters' robes snapping in the shockwave.

[Camera] Wide side profile, eye level, one whip-pan following the draw, ending frame: locked blades center frame, toppling bamboo frozen mid-fall at the edges, sparks hanging in the air.

[Look] Cool dawn key through fog, high-contrast blade glints, speed lines on the draw only. Bamboo greens desaturated, crimson and teal costumes saturated for contrast. Impact frames: two white flash frames at blade contact.

[Sound] Ambience: wind through bamboo, leaf rustle. Action sound: twin draws ringing, bamboo cracking and thudding, blade lock shriek. Music: taiko hit on the leaf landing, driving strings under the exchange. Dialogue: none.

[Constraints] Must remain: Ren's twin blade design, Kaze's katana design, ponytail and topknot, 16:9. Likely failure modes: blades bend or change length mid-swing; one fighter freezes while the other moves; bamboo breaks without a blade touching it; sparks render as static dots. Reject any take where a blade visibly morphs.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 2: Technique Unleash, Crater Field

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, 2D anime style, clean linework, dynamic shading.

[Subject] MIRA as defined in the character lock (short silver hair, black and gold battle dress, staff with a ringed head, locked design). AZURE FLAME technique: locked design, blue-white core, white-gold edges, rising spark spirals, no smoke.

[World] Night, shattered crater field, cracked stone under a starless sky. Broken pillars foreground, dust hanging midground, a distant burning treeline on the horizon.

[Action] Trigger: Mira slams the staff's ringed head into the cracked stone. Continuous motion: azure flame erupts from the impact point in a rising spiral, stone shards lifting and orbiting the column, her hair and dress whipping upward, the spiral tightening as she raises the staff overhead. Visible result: she brings the staff down, and the flame column collapses into a single horizontal lance that streaks toward the burning treeline, the horizon flaring white.

[Camera] Low angle wide, one rising crane path from ground level to above the flame column, ending frame: the lance streaking away as a horizontal line of azure light, Mira small beneath it, staff still raised.

[Look] Azure flame as the key light, deep blue-black shadows, stone rendered in cool greys. Heavy glow on the flame core with clean edges, no muddy bloom. Slow-motion on the staff raise (half speed for one second), full speed on the lance release.

[Sound] Ambience: wind over cracked stone, distant fire crackle. Action sound: staff slam boom, flame roar rising in pitch, lance release like a thunderclap. Music: choir swell under the spiral, cut to silence on the release. Dialogue: MIRA (rising): "Azure Flame, Ninth Form." Then the slam.

[Constraints] Must remain: azure flame locked design (blue-white core, white-gold edges, spark spirals), staff ringed head, 16:9. Likely failure modes: the flame changes color mid-shot (blue to orange); the staff bends; stone shards freeze mid-air; the lance curves without cause. Reject any take where the flame color drifts from the lock.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 3: Transformation, Moonlit Ruin

[Output] 9 seconds, 16:9, 2D anime style, clean linework, dynamic shading.

[Subject] DAIN as defined in the character lock (before: ragged grey tunic, barefoot, shaggy brown hair; after: silver-streaked hair standing upright, black scaled armor forming over the tunic, ember-glow seams, locked design).

[World] Midnight, collapsed temple courtyard, full moon through broken columns. Rubble foreground, shattered altar midground, moon disc huge in the background sky. Dust motes drifting in the moonbeams.

[Action] Trigger: Dain drives his fist into the altar stone, cracking it. Continuous motion: ember light bleeds from the crack up his arm, black scales rippling outward across his shoulders and chest, his hair lifting and silvering strand by strand, dust and small stones lifting off the ground in a rising ring. Visible result: the transformation completes as he stands upright, ember seams pulsing once along the armor, and the shockwave ring slams outward, toppling two columns.

[Camera] Medium shot, eye level, one slow orbit of 90 degrees around Dain, ending frame: front three-quarter view, armor complete, ember seams glowing, columns falling at the frame edges.

[Look] Cold moonlight key, ember orange as the only warm source, deep blue shadows. Armor scales with hard specular edges. Energy distortion rippling the air around him. Transformation rendered as continuous motion, never a cut to a new design.

[Sound] Ambience: night wind through the ruin, settling stone. Action sound: altar crack, scale formation like cooling metal ticking, shockwave boom. Music: low drone rising to a single brass hit on completion. Dialogue: DAIN (low, through the change): "Enough running."

[Constraints] Must remain: before/after designs exactly as locked, ember seam glow color, 16:9. Likely failure modes: the armor pops in via a hidden cut instead of forming continuously; hair changes style between frames; proportions shift (shoulders widen then narrow); the moon disc wobbles. Reject any take where the armor appears without the rippling formation.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 4: Team Clash, Burning Bridge

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, 2D anime style, clean linework, dynamic shading.

[Subject] TEAM A as defined in the character lock (three fighters, blue sashes, locked weapon designs). TEAM B as defined in the character lock (three fighters, red sashes, locked weapon designs).

[World] Sunset, rope bridge over a gorge, the bridge deck on fire at the far end. Burning planks foreground, fraying ropes midground, gorge mist below, orange sun low behind the far cliff.

[Action] Trigger: the lead fighter of Team B charges across the burning deck. Continuous motion: both teams collide mid-bridge, blades and staves crossing in continuous exchanges, the bridge swaying and planks snapping underfoot, burning debris falling into the mist, all six fighters in motion with no one standing idle. Visible result: a plank gives way under two fighters, and they drop together, weapons still locked, into the gorge mist.

[Camera] Wide tracking shot along the bridge's length, one lateral move following the clash, ending frame: the empty gap in the bridge deck, two falling silhouettes shrinking into the mist, the remaining four still fighting.

[Look] Sunset orange key through fire smoke, silhouettes rimmed in gold, fire glow from below the deck. Heavy motion blur on weapon arcs, embers swirling. Sway of the bridge synced to the fighters' steps.

[Sound] Ambience: gorge wind, fire roar. Action sound: wood cracking, rope creaking, blade impacts, debris hissing into mist. Music: driving percussion, no melody. Dialogue: none, battle cries only.

[Constraints] Must remain: blue vs red sashes for team readability, all six weapon designs locked, 16:9. Likely failure modes: fighters swap teams (sash colors drift); background fighters freeze while foreground fights; weapons pass through bodies; the bridge stops swaying. Reject any take where any fighter stands motionless for more than a second.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 5: Rooftop Chase, Rain

[Output] 7 seconds, 16:9, 2D anime style, clean linework, dynamic shading.

[Subject] YUKI as defined in the character lock (short white hair, black courier jacket, twin daggers sheathed at the hips, locked design). HUNTER as defined in the character lock (long coat, wide-brim hat, chain whip, locked design).

[World] Night, rain-soaked city rooftops, neon signs below. AC units and antenna arrays foreground, slick roof tiles midground, neon glow bleeding up from the streets. Rain streaking diagonally in the wind.

[Action] Trigger: the hunter's chain whip lashes out, shattering an antenna behind Yuki. Continuous motion: Yuki sprints and leaps a six-foot gap between roofs, coat flaring, rain sheeting off her; the hunter follows, chain whip whirling overhead, both landing in rolls and coming up running without breaking stride. Visible result: Yuki vaults an AC unit and kicks off a billboard frame, gaining a full rooftop of distance as the whip cracks where she stood.

[Camera] Dynamic side track, slightly low angle, one continuous lateral move matching their speed across two roofs, ending frame: Yuki mid-kick off the billboard frame, the hunter a roof behind, chain whip extended.

[Look] Neon magenta and cyan rim light on rain-slick surfaces, deep blue night shadows, rain rendered as diagonal streaks with splash bursts on impact. Speed lines on the leaps. Wet cloth with specular streaks.

[Sound] Ambience: rain hammer, distant neon buzz. Action sound: antenna shatter, boots on wet tile, chain whip cracks, billboard frame clang. Music: electronic pulse matching footfalls. Dialogue: none.

[Constraints] Must remain: Yuki's white hair and courier jacket, hunter's hat and chain whip design, 16:9. Likely failure modes: the gap jump becomes a float (no arc, no landing impact); rain stops mid-shot; the chain whip changes length; rooftops merge into one flat plane. Reject any take where a leap has no visible landing.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.
