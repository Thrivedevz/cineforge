# Fantasy-Film Directing Briefs (Photorealistic Original Micro-Films)

Copy-ready scene briefs for photorealistic ORIGINAL fantasy micro-films in the MYTHRA style: fully AI-generated, cinematic, story-driven. Every brief follows the 8-block formula. All are model-agnostic.

## House Rules (apply to every brief below)

- Continuous motion in every shot. No slideshow feel: something is always moving (characters, cloth, water, embers, mist). A static frame is a failed frame.
- Natural, continuous narration and dialogue with no long dead-air pauses. Lines flow like speech, not like captions with gaps. If a pause is needed, it is one beat, filled by sound design.
- Seamless short dissolves between shots, not hard cuts, so scenes melt into each other.
- Continuous background audio across the whole film: the ambience bed never drops to silence between shots.
- Unified cinematic finish: one grade, one grain, one motion treatment across every shot of the film.
- Multi-voice where dialogue occurs: distinct voices per character, cast in the voiceover block, never one voice doing everyone.

## Brief 1: Golem Awakening

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic dark fantasy.

[Subject] GOLEM as defined in the character lock (nine feet tall, river-stone body, moss seams, amber eyes that ignite on waking, locked design). ELDER as defined in the character lock (grey robes, oak staff, voice: low female).

[World] Pre-dawn, stone circle on a cliffside, sea mist rolling in. Standing stones foreground, the golem kneeling center as a moss-covered boulder, cliff edge and grey sea behind. Cold blue half-light.

[Action] Trigger: the elder presses her palm to the golem's brow stone and speaks. Continuous motion: amber light ignites behind the stone eyes, moss seams cracking as the head lifts, shoulders rising, one massive hand planting on the ground and pushing the torso upright, mist parting around the rising form. Visible result: the golem stands at full height, amber eyes flaring once, and turns its head toward the elder.

[Camera] Low angle medium wide, one slow crane up matching the golem's rise, ending frame: the golem towering over the elder, sea mist behind, amber eyes level with camera.

[Look] Cold blue pre-dawn key, amber eye-glow as the only warm source, desaturated stone greys. Moss with wet texture. Heavy atmosphere: mist never still. Subtle film grain across the shot.

[Sound] Ambience: sea wind, distant surf, continuous bed under the whole film. Action sound: stone grinding, moss tearing, deep resonant hum as the eyes ignite. Music: low strings entering on the head lift. Dialogue: ELDER (low female, steady): "Rise, keeper. The tide has turned." No pause longer than one beat before the stone answers with motion.

[Constraints] Must remain: golem stone-body design, amber eye color, elder's grey robes, 16:9. Likely failure modes: the golem's proportions shift between kneeling and standing; the eyes ignite then change color; the rise happens as a hidden cut; mist freezes. Reject any take where the golem stands up off-camera or between cuts.

## Brief 2: Forest Spirit

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic dark fantasy.

[Subject] SPIRIT as defined in the character lock (tall and slender, bark-textured skin, antler crown with hanging moss, firefly-light drifting off the body, locked design). CHILD as defined in the character lock (small, red scarf, lantern, voice: young, clear).

[World] Night, ancient hollow beneath a great root system, bioluminescent fungi. Massive roots foreground framing the hollow, the spirit emerging from the dark between them, fungi glowing teal midground. Fireflies already in the air.

[Action] Trigger: the child's lantern swings, its light catching two eyes in the dark. Continuous motion: the spirit steps forward out of the roots, each footfall stirring a swirl of fireflies, moss on the antlers swaying, one long arm extending toward the lantern, firefly-light trailing off the fingertips. Visible result: the spirit's hand closes gently around the lantern's glow without touching the metal, and the light flares teal-green once.

[Camera] Medium shot, eye level, one slow push-in toward the lantern, ending frame: the spirit's hand cupped around the glowing lantern, the child's small hand still on the handle, both lit teal-green.

[Look] Near-black shadows, teal fungi glow as key, warm lantern light as fill, firefly points of gold. Bark skin with deep texture. Shallow depth of field on the hand and lantern. Continuous drifting motion in every light source.

[Sound] Ambience: night insects, dripping water, continuous bed. Action sound: soft footfalls on loam, firefly hum rising, lantern glass ticking as it warms. Music: sparse wooden flute phrase under the approach. Dialogue: CHILD (young, clear, wonder, no fear): "Are you the one the trees talk about?" SPIRIT (layered whisper, multi-voice effect): "The trees talk about many things, little light." Lines land back to back, no dead air.

[Constraints] Must remain: spirit bark-skin and antler design, firefly-light behavior, 16:9. Likely failure modes: the spirit's limbs stretch or duplicate; fireflies freeze into static dots; the lantern light changes color; the spirit's face renders human. Reject any take where the fireflies stop moving.

## Brief 3: Mountain Titan

[Output] 9 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic dark fantasy.

[Subject] TITAN as defined in the character lock (a walking mountain: granite shoulders, waterfall beard, pine forests on the back, eyes like quarry lamps, locked design). No human characters in this shot.

[World] Dawn, high alpine valley, storm light breaking through. Scree slopes foreground, the titan crossing midground left to right, snow peaks behind. Wind driving cloud shadows across the valley.

[Action] Trigger: a rockfall starts on the slope above as the titan's foot lands. Continuous motion: the titan strides across the valley, each footfall shaking loose scree and sending up dust plumes, the waterfall beard flowing uninterrupted, pines on its back swaying, cloud shadows racing. Visible result: the titan reaches the far ridge, pauses one beat, and its quarry-lamp eyes sweep once across the valley below.

[Camera] Extreme wide, slightly low angle, one slow lateral track holding the titan in frame, ending frame: the titan silhouetted on the far ridge against the breaking dawn, valley spread below.

[Look] Storm-break dawn: hard gold shafts through bruised cloud, granite rendered in cold greys with warm rim light. Waterfall with continuous white motion. Massive scale held by slow, heavy movement: nothing about the titan moves fast.

[Sound] Ambience: alpine wind, continuous bed. Action sound: deep footfall booms with rockfall rattle, waterfall roar constant. Music: massive low brass on each footfall, choir under the eye sweep. Dialogue: none.

[Constraints] Must remain: titan granite/pine/waterfall design, quarry-lamp eye color, 16:9. Likely failure modes: the titan's stride speeds up to human pace (scale breaks); the waterfall freezes; pines on the back detach or slide; footfalls and sound desync. Reject any take where the titan moves at human speed.

## Brief 4: Tide Witch

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic dark fantasy.

[Subject] WITCH as defined in the character lock (tall, black kelp-woven cloak, driftwood staff topped with a fishing float, bioluminescent tide marks on the hands, locked design). Sailor voices: two distinct male voices off-camera.

[World] Night, black-sand beach at low tide, phosphorescent surf. Wet sand mirror foreground, the witch standing at the waterline midground, wreck ribs of an old ship behind her. Moon hidden, glow coming from the water itself.

[Action] Trigger: the witch raises the driftwood staff overhead. Continuous motion: the phosphorescent surf surges forward in a glowing wall, her kelp cloak streaming in the wind, tide marks on her hands brightening, the wave curling and then parting around her in two glowing arcs that race up the beach. Visible result: the arcs collapse back into the sea, leaving a circle of glowing foam around her boots, and she lowers the staff.

[Camera] Medium wide, eye level, one slow push-in that stops as the wave parts, ending frame: the witch centered in the glowing foam circle, staff lowered, wreck ribs framing her.

[Look] Phosphor teal-green as the key light, black sand with mirror reflections, deep blue-black sky. Kelp cloak with wet sheen. Water in constant motion: the sea never holds still in frame.

[Sound] Ambience: surf and night wind, continuous bed. Action sound: wave surge roar, foam hiss, staff humming as it rises. Music: deep female choir under the surge. Dialogue: SAILOR 1 (off-camera, rough male, urgent): "She's calling the tide." SAILOR 2 (off-camera, younger male, hushed): "Then we run." Lines overlap the wave's rise, no pause between them.

[Constraints] Must remain: witch kelp-cloak and staff design, phosphor color, 16:9. Likely failure modes: the wave renders as a static glowing wall; the cloak clips through her body; tide marks change pattern; the foam circle appears before the wave collapses. Reject any take where the water surface holds still.

## Brief 5: Ember Knight

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic dark fantasy.

[Subject] KNIGHT as defined in the character lock (blackened plate armor with ember cracks, greatsword with a smoldering edge, plume of ash, locked design). SQUIRE as defined in the character lock (leather jerkin, ash-smudged face, voice: earnest young male).

[World] Dusk, battlefield aftermath, embers drifting. Fallen banners foreground, the knight walking through the ash midground, a burning treeline on the horizon. Heat shimmer rising everywhere.

[Action] Trigger: the squire runs in from the side, skidding to a stop. Continuous motion: the knight keeps walking forward through the ash, embers swirling around the armor, the greatsword dragging a smoldering line in the ash behind; the squire falls into step beside him, matching the pace, ash puffing under both their boots. Visible result: the knight stops, plants the greatsword point-first in the ash, and rests both hands on the pommel, embers spiraling up around the blade.

[Camera] Medium wide lateral track, eye level, one continuous move holding both in frame, ending frame: the knight leaning on the planted greatsword, the squire beside him, burning horizon behind.

[Look] Ember orange key against blue-grey dusk, armor black with glowing crack lines, ash with fine texture. Heat shimmer distortion on the horizon. Embers in constant drift: the air is never empty.

[Sound] Ambience: wind over ash, distant fire crackle, continuous bed. Action sound: armored footfalls, greatsword dragging, ash crunch under the squire's boots. Music: mournful cello line under the walk. Dialogue: SQUIRE (earnest young male, breathless, falling into step): "The line holds, ser. They are asking for you." KNIGHT (deep male, gravel, without stopping): "Then they will wait." Beat. Then he plants the sword.

[Constraints] Must remain: knight armor and ember-crack design, greatsword smoldering edge, 16:9. Likely failure modes: ember cracks change pattern or color; the sword's drag line appears ahead of the blade; the squire's pace desyncs; embers freeze. Reject any take where the armor's ember pattern differs from the lock.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.
