## Brief 1: Alley Chase

[Output] 7 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic live-action look.

[Subject] COURIER as defined in the character lock (mid 20s, short black hair, navy windbreaker, black backpack, white sneakers). PURSUER as defined in the character lock (tall, shaved head, dark leather jacket).

[World] Late afternoon, narrow brick alley, overcast with light drizzle. Dumpsters foreground left, fire escapes midground, chain-link gate at the far end. Puddles on asphalt reflecting grey sky.

[Action] Trigger: the pursuer's hand slams a dumpster lid shut behind them. Continuous motion: the courier sprints toward camera down the alley, backpack bouncing, water spraying off each footstep, the pursuer gaining three paces behind. Visible result: the courier vaults the chain-link gate, landing hard on the far side, and keeps running as the pursuer grabs the fence.

[Camera] Low angle wide, handheld with lateral shake, one push-in path toward the gate, ending frame: the pursuer gripping the chain-link, the courier already a blur at the far end of the next street.

[Look] Flat overcast daylight, cool grey-blue grade, wet asphalt reflections. Brick texture rough and damp. Handheld micro shake, slight motion blur on the sprint. No slow motion: speed reads as real.

[Sound] Ambience: rain hiss, distant traffic. Action sound: footsteps slapping wet asphalt, dumpster lid bang, chain-link rattling on the vault. Music: low pulse drone under the chase. Dialogue: none.

[Constraints] Must remain: courier's navy windbreaker, pursuer's leather jacket, 16:9. Likely failure modes: generator freezes both into running poses with no motion; the fence warps on the vault; faces distort at speed; rain vanishes. Reject any take where either character stops moving.

[Action] Trigger: Marcus sets his mug down, the ceramic clicking the table. Visible result: Marcus reaches across, covers her hand with his, and she exhales once, her shoulders dropping a fraction. [Sound] Ambience: rain on glass, refrigerator hum. Action sound: mug clicking the table, kettle faint whistle under. Music: none. Dialogue: MARCUS (soft): "You don't have to carry it alone." Beat of silence. Then the hand covers hers.

[Constraints] Must remain: Elaine's silver bun, Marcus's rolled sleeves, warm-cool light contrast, 16:9. Likely failure modes: extra fingers in the hand overlap; faces drift between cuts; steam freezes; the push-in speeds up. Reject any take where the dialogue overlaps the hand movement.
