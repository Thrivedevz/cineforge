# Ads and UGC Directing Briefs

Copy-ready scene briefs for product ads and UGC persona spots. Every brief follows the 8-block formula. All are model-agnostic.

UGC note: these briefs direct a believable person-on-camera feel (natural light, slight handheld, unpolished delivery). The persona speaks to camera like a real user, not an announcer. Keep takes short, keep motion real, keep the product the hero of the frame.

---

## Brief 1: Beverage, Morning Ritual

[Output] 6 seconds, 9:16, photorealistic, bright natural light.

[Subject] PERSONA as defined in the character lock (late 20s, messy bun, oversized sweater). PRODUCT: cold brew can, matte black with a copper band, locked label design.

[World] 7:30 AM, small sunny kitchen, steam and morning light. Window with sheer curtains foreground right, wooden counter midground, kettle and plant in the background. Dust motes in the sunbeam.

[Action] Trigger: the persona cracks the can open, the tab hissing. Continuous motion: she pours the cold brew over ice in a clear glass, the dark liquid swirling, condensation blooming on the glass, then lifts the glass toward camera. Visible result: she takes a sip, eyes closing for one beat, and smiles at camera holding the glass up.

[Camera] Medium close-up, eye level, slight handheld sway, one push-in toward the glass during the pour, ending frame: the glass held up center frame, condensation sharp, her smile soft behind it.

[Look] Warm morning sun key through the sheer curtain, bright and airy grade, whites clean. Condensation with macro detail. Natural handheld: present but not shaky.

[Sound] Ambience: morning birds, kettle click. Action sound: can crack hiss, liquid pour over ice, ice clinking. Music: light acoustic guitar motif. Dialogue: PERSONA (warm, to camera, while lifting the glass): "Okay, this is my whole morning now."

[Constraints] Must remain: can label design (matte black, copper band), 9:16. Likely failure modes: the label text garbles or changes; liquid pours without the can tilting; condensation appears before the pour; her face distorts on the smile. Reject any take where the label is unreadable or wrong.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 2: Skincare, Bathroom Mirror

[Output] 6 seconds, 9:16, photorealistic, soft daylight.

[Subject] PERSONA as defined in the character lock (early 30s, bare face, hair clipped back). PRODUCT: serum bottle, frosted glass with a white dropper cap, locked label design.

[World] 8:00 AM, bright bathroom, soft window light. Mirror foreground (camera shoots the reflection), marble sink midground, eucalyptus sprig in a vase behind. Clean, uncluttered.

[Action] Trigger: the persona picks up the serum bottle and holds it to camera. Continuous motion: she unscrews the dropper, draws serum up, releases three drops onto her cheek, and presses it in with her fingertips in slow circles, the skin catching the light. Visible result: she lowers her hands, turns her cheek to the light, and the skin shows a visible dewy sheen.

[Camera] Medium close-up on the mirror reflection, eye level, locked on a tripod with the tiniest drift, ending frame: her cheek turned to the window light, serum bottle sharp in her hand foreground.

[Look] Soft diffused daylight, clean bright grade, skin tones true. Dewy highlight on the cheek as the payoff. No beauty filter look: texture stays real.

[Sound] Ambience: quiet room tone, faint water drip. Action sound: dropper unscrewing, glass dropper clink, soft fingertip pats. Music: none, intimate quiet. Dialogue: PERSONA (soft, to camera, while pressing the serum in): "Three drops. That is the whole routine."

[Constraints] Must remain: bottle label design, 9:16. Likely failure modes: dropper separates from the bottle; drops fall without the dropper squeezing; fingers sink into the cheek; the label mirrors backwards in the reflection (check: labels read correctly in mirror shots or the take is rejected). Reject any take with mirrored label text.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 3: Headphones, Commute Drop

[Output] 7 seconds, 9:16, photorealistic, urban daylight.

[Subject] PERSONA as defined in the character lock (mid 20s, streetwear, backpack). PRODUCT: over-ear headphones, matte charcoal with copper hinges, locked design.

[World] 8:15 AM, subway platform, train arriving. Yellow platform line foreground, the train sliding in midground, commuters blurred behind. Fluorescent platform light with daylight from the street grate.

[Action] Trigger: the train doors chime. Continuous motion: the persona lifts the headphones from around the neck, settles them over the ears in one motion, taps the right earcup once, and the head starts nodding to the beat as the train pulls in, commuters streaming past in the background. Visible result: close on the persona, eyes closed, fully in the music, the platform noise visibly shut out.

[Camera] Medium shot, eye level, one lateral track following the persona along the platform, ending frame: tight on the headphones and the nodding head, train blurred behind.

[Look] Urban fluorescent mixed with daylight, slightly contrasty grade, charcoal headphones matte against skin. Background commuters in soft motion blur. Handheld with purposeful sway.

[Sound] Ambience: platform rumble, door chime, crowd murmur (all ducking under the tap). Action sound: earcup tap click, then the persona's music takes over the mix. Music: punchy beat drop on the tap. Dialogue: PERSONA (to camera, before the tap): "Watch this." Tap. Then the music.

[Constraints] Must remain: headphone design (charcoal, copper hinges), 9:16. Likely failure modes: headphones change shape when lifted; the earcup tap lands on empty air; the head nod desyncs from the beat; commuters freeze mid-stride. Reject any take where the headphones morph between the lift and the wear.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 4: App Demo, Couch Walkthrough

[Output] 8 seconds, 9:16, photorealistic, warm interior light.

[Subject] PERSONA as defined in the character lock (30s, casual tee, natural delivery). PRODUCT: phone screen showing the app, UI screens provided as locked reference frames.

[World] 7:00 PM, living room couch, warm lamp light. Coffee table foreground, the persona on the couch midground, bookshelf behind. Cozy, lived-in.

[Action] Trigger: the persona holds the phone up to camera, screen facing out. Continuous motion: the thumb taps through three app screens (home, detail, checkout), each transition smooth, the persona's eyes flicking between screen and camera, a nod on the checkout screen. Visible result: the persona flips the phone toward camera on the confirmation screen and grins.

[Camera] Over-the-shoulder into a front medium shot, eye level, locked tripod with reframing only on the screen taps, ending frame: the confirmation screen filling the lower half of frame, the persona's grin above it.

[Look] Warm lamp key, soft shadows, screen glow lighting the persona's face. UI screens bright and legible. Stable frame: the phone is the moving element, not the camera.

[Sound] Ambience: quiet room tone, distant TV murmur. Action sound: screen taps, UI swooshes. Music: subtle upbeat bed under the demo. Dialogue: PERSONA (natural, to camera, tapping through): "Home, detail, checkout. Done in like ten seconds." Then the grin.

[Constraints] Must remain: UI screens match the locked reference frames exactly, 9:16. Likely failure modes: UI text garbles or invents new screens; the thumb taps without the screen changing; the phone changes model mid-shot; screen glare whites out the UI. Reject any take where the UI deviates from the reference frames.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 5: Unboxing, Desk Setup

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic, bright desk light.

[Subject] PERSONA as defined in the character lock (20s, hoodie, hands only in the opening beats, face revealed at the end). PRODUCT: minimalist desk lamp in packaging, box with locked branding.

[World] 2:00 PM, clean desk by a window, daylight. Box center frame foreground, scissors and tissue paper midground, shelf with plants behind. Bright, minimal.

[Action] Trigger: the persona slices the box tape with scissors. Continuous motion: hands open the flaps, lift out tissue paper, reveal the lamp nested in molded packaging, lift the lamp free, and set it on the desk; a hand reaches in frame and clicks it on. Visible result: the lamp glows warm on the desk, the persona leans into frame smiling, packaging pushed aside.

[Camera] Top-down into a front medium shot, one tilt path from overhead to eye level as the lamp is lifted, ending frame: the lit lamp on the desk, the persona's smile behind it, warm pool of light on the wood.

[Look] Bright daylight key, warm lamp glow as the payoff accent, clean whites. Packaging textures crisp. Smooth tilt, no shake: the unboxing is the motion.

[Sound] Ambience: quiet room, distant street. Action sound: tape slice, cardboard flaps, tissue rustle, lamp click. Music: bright plucky motif under the reveal. Dialogue: PERSONA (leaning in, delighted): "Oh, that glow is perfect."

[Constraints] Must remain: box branding, lamp design, 16:9. Likely failure modes: hands gain extra fingers in close-up; the lamp changes shape between packaging and desk; tissue paper phases through the box; the lamp lights before the click. Reject any take with hand anatomy errors.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.
