# Documentary Directing Briefs

Copy-ready scene briefs for documentary footage: nature, craft, urban, and science explainers. Observational tone throughout: the camera watches, it does not perform. Every brief follows the 8-block formula. All are model-agnostic.

---

## Brief 1: Nature, Heron at Dawn

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic natural history.

[Subject] GREY HERON, adult, slate-grey plumage, yellow bill, locked markings (dark shoulder patches, white crown stripe).

[World] 5:50 AM, freshwater marsh, mist on the water. Reeds foreground, the heron standing mid-leg in still water midground, willow silhouette behind. First light pink on the mist.

[Action] Trigger: a ripple moves through the reeds toward the heron. Continuous motion: the heron freezes, neck coiling back in the strike posture, then the bill spears down in a blur, water erupting in a small crown of droplets. Visible result: the heron rises with a silver fish writhing in the bill, tosses its head once, and swallows.

[Camera] Long lens medium shot, eye level from a hide, locked tripod, no movement until the strike, then one subtle reframe to hold the heron, ending frame: the heron settling back to stillness, ripples spreading.

[Look] Dawn pink-gold key through mist, soft and low contrast, natural color. Water with mirror clarity before the strike. No grade tricks: the scene sells itself.

[Sound] Ambience: dawn chorus, water lap, reed rustle. Action sound: the spear splash, fish thrash, single gulp. Music: none. Narration (calm, observational, after the swallow): "Ninety milliseconds. The marsh does not get a second chance."

[Constraints] Must remain: heron markings, natural behavior (no anthropomorphism), 16:9. Likely failure modes: the fish appears before the strike; the heron's neck bends impossibly; water stays glassy after the splash; the strike renders in slow motion without cause. Reject any take where the fish is visible before the bill hits the water.

## Brief 2: Craft, Potter's Hands

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic craft documentary.

[Subject] POTTER as defined in the character lock (60s, rolled sleeves, clay-stained apron, hands as the focus). Wheel and clay: locked setup, wooden wheel, grey stoneware clay.

[World] 10:00 AM, pottery studio, north window light. Wheel foreground, the potter's torso midground, shelves of unfired pots behind. Clay slurry and tools on the bench.

[Action] Trigger: the potter's foot starts the wheel treadle. Continuous motion: wet hands center the spinning clay, thumbs pressing the opening, fingers drawing the wall upward in one continuous pull, water sheeting off the form. Visible result: the cylinder steadies into a tall vessel, the potter's hands lifting away clean, the wheel slowing.

[Camera] Tight on the hands and clay, slightly high angle, locked tripod, one slow push-in across the pull, ending frame: the finished vessel spinning slowly, hands withdrawn, water droplets on the rim.

[Look] Soft north-window key, gentle shadows, honest color. Wet clay with specular sheen. Shallow depth of field on the hands. No beautification: clay under the nails stays.

[Sound] Ambience: studio quiet, distant traffic. Action sound: wheel hum, wet clay squelch, water dripping. Music: none. Narration (warm, observational, over the pull): "Forty years, and the clay still decides."

[Constraints] Must remain: the potter's hands (no hand doubles, no jewelry appearing), 16:9. Likely failure modes: extra fingers in close-up; the clay changes shape without the hands touching it; the wheel spins but the clay stays still; water renders as static shine. Reject any take with hand anatomy errors.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 3: Urban, Night Market

[Output] 9 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic urban documentary.

[Subject] VENDOR as defined in the character lock (40s, apron, wok station). Crowd: real passersby, no staged extras feel.

[World] 9:30 PM, night market lane, steam and neon. Wok station foreground, the vendor midground, crowd flowing behind, hanging bulbs and signs above. Steam catching the light everywhere.

[Action] Trigger: the vendor tosses the wok, flames flaring. Continuous motion: the wok arcs through the flame, ingredients tumbling in the air and landing back in the pan, the vendor's other hand plating the previous order, steam billowing, the crowd flowing past without stopping. Visible result: the vendor slides the finished plate across the counter to a waiting hand, already turning back to the wok.

[Camera] Medium wide, eye level, one slow lateral drift along the counter, ending frame: the plate sliding into the customer's hand, the wok already back over the flame.

[Look] Tungsten bulb key, neon accents from the signs, steam backlit. Warm, slightly contrasty grade. Crowd in natural motion blur. Handheld with documentary sway.

[Sound] Ambience: crowd murmur, sizzle bed, distant music from a stall. Action sound: wok clang, flame whoosh, plate slide. Music: none, the market is the music. Narration (observational, over the toss): "Three hundred plates a night. The wok never cools."

[Constraints] Must remain: vendor's apron and station setup, real crowd behavior (no one looks at camera), 16:9. Likely failure modes: the crowd freezes; flames render as a static overlay; ingredients hang in the air; a passerby stares into the lens. Reject any take where anyone looks at the camera.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 4: Science Explainer, Ferrofluid

[Output] 8 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic science explainer, macro.

[Subject] FERROFLUID in a shallow glass dish, black liquid, locked behavior (spikes form under magnetic field, collapse when it lifts).

[World] Lab bench, controlled light, dark background. Dish center frame foreground, a hand holding a magnet above it midground, out-of-focus lab glassware behind. Clean and minimal.

[Action] Trigger: the hand lowers the magnet toward the dish. Continuous motion: the black liquid rises into sharp spikes that track the magnet, the hand moving the magnet in a slow circle and the spike field following, peaks splitting and reforming. Visible result: the hand lifts the magnet away, and the spikes collapse back into a flat black mirror.

[Camera] Macro, slightly high angle, locked tripod, no movement: the fluid is the motion, ending frame: the flat black surface, the magnet hovering just out of frame top.

[Look] Hard key light on the spikes for definition, dark background for contrast, spikes with glossy specular peaks. True black fluid, no color shift. Clinical and precise.

[Sound] Ambience: lab quiet, ventilation hum. Action sound: faint magnetic hum rising with the spikes. Music: none. Narration (clear, explanatory, over the spike formation): "Each spike is the fluid finding the shortest path through the field. Move the magnet, and the landscape follows."

[Constraints] Must remain: true black fluid, spike behavior tied to the magnet's position, 16:9. Likely failure modes: spikes form before the magnet arrives; spikes detach and float; the fluid changes color; the hand's magnet passes through the dish. Reject any take where the spikes move independently of the magnet.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.

## Brief 5: Nature, Starling Murmuration

[Output] 9 seconds, 16:9, photorealistic natural history.

[Subject] STARLING FLOCK, several thousand birds, moving as one murmuration.

[World] 6:10 PM, winter, reed bed at a wetland edge. Reeds foreground, the flock midground against a pale sunset sky, a distant tree line behind. Cold still air.

[Action] Trigger: a peregrine's shadow crosses the flock's edge. Continuous motion: the murmuration folds in on itself, ribboning into a tight ball, then unfurling into a sweeping arc, the shape pulsing and reforming without a single bird breaking formation. Visible result: the flock pours down into the reed bed in a dark waterfall, vanishing into the reeds as the last light fades.

[Camera] Wide, eye level, locked tripod, no movement: the sky is the subject and the frame holds it, ending frame: empty sunset sky, reeds settling, a few late birds dropping in.

[Look] Pale winter sunset key, birds as dark calligraphy against the sky, natural color. No slow motion: the speed is the spectacle.

[Sound] Ambience: wind in reeds, distant water. Action sound: the flock's wing rush swelling and fading. Music: none. Narration (quiet, observational, as the flock folds): "No leader. No signal. Just ten thousand neighbors, each watching seven others."

[Constraints] Must remain: natural flock behavior (no impossible shapes like letters or logos), 16:9. Likely failure modes: the flock freezes into a static shape; birds detach and hover; the murmuration forms a recognizable symbol; the peregrine is never visible yet the flock reacts to nothing. Reject any take where the flock holds a fixed shape for more than a second.

Last verified: 2026-10. Model-agnostic.
